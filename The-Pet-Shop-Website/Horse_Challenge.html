Horses are strong and intelligent animals often used for riding and farming.

Horses are herbivores, which means they eat plants. They have a unique digestive system that allows them to extract nutrients from tough plant material. Horses are social animals and often form strong bonds with other horses and humans. They communicate through body language, vocalizations, and facial expressions.
